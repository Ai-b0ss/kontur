# Как созданы исследовательские листы

Дата: 6 августа 2026 года  
Способ: встроенный генератор изображений Codex  
Режим: создание новых изображений без входных изображений  
Назначение: только выбор художественного направления, не production-материалы

## А · «Живое гнездо»

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: exploratory art-direction concept sheet for a Telegram Mini App, direction A
Primary request: create one polished landscape concept sheet for an original youth community guide called Kontur. Show a bird companion that represents a person's path into a useful conversation, not a profession or ranking.
Scene/backdrop: warm off-white recycled-paper field with subtle fibers; no forest illustration, only abstract nest threads and small paper fragments.
Subject: one distinctive compact bird design with an asymmetrical soft silhouette, expressive posture, intelligent but not human face. Show one large hero bird and four smaller sequential poses: cautiously observing, finding a thread, arranging a simple nest, connecting two threads. Include a small material/palette corner and a four-frame motion storyboard where a loose thread gently guides the bird and settles into a nest.
Style/medium: contemporary editorial illustration, tactile cut paper, soft colored-pencil grain, restrained geometric shapes, premium art-school portfolio quality.
Composition/framing: landscape presentation board, generous whitespace, clearly separated character study, progression poses, material samples and motion frames; no device mockup.
Lighting/mood: calm, welcoming, curious, quietly witty; never childish.
Color palette: warm cream, burnt coral, deep plum, moss green, tiny amber accent.
Constraints: original design only; no logos; no university marks; no text or letters; no watermark; each of the four progression poses must be visibly the same bird; readable at small mobile scale.
Avoid: generic round mobile-game mascot, Disney/Pixar look, baby chick, glossy 3D, corporate gradients, fantasy forest, crowns, badges, coins, leaderboards, professional accessories, school uniform, cute overload.
```

## Б · «Карта сигналов»

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: exploratory art-direction concept sheet for a Telegram Mini App, direction B
Primary request: create one polished landscape concept sheet for an original youth community guide called Kontur. Show a bird companion as a navigator through conversation signals and saved context, never as a profession, score, or hierarchy.
Scene/backdrop: pale mineral gray technical field with a subtle modular grid, thin route lines and abstract message nodes.
Subject: an original angular bird built from a few bold planar shapes, long attentive neck and distinct folded wing, expressive through tilt and balance rather than facial cuteness. Show one large hero bird and four smaller sequential poses: scanning several signal dots, selecting one luminous thread, folding the thread into an orderly nest, opening a bridge between two nodes. Include a palette/material corner and a four-frame motion storyboard showing a dot pulse becoming a line, then a nest contour.
Style/medium: crisp editorial vector-like illustration with screen-print texture, modern information design, experimental technology-school identity, precise but human.
Composition/framing: landscape presentation board, clear zones for character study, four progression poses, palette/material samples and motion frames; no phone frame and no readable labels.
Lighting/mood: alert, capable, clear, optimistic without hype.
Color palette: chalk gray, nearly black navy, electric cobalt, sharp chartreuse, one warm coral accent.
Constraints: original design only; no logos; no university marks; no text or letters; no watermark; the four states must clearly be the same bird; strong silhouette at small mobile scale.
Avoid: generic startup gradient, cyberpunk neon darkness, robot bird, owl cliché, glossy 3D, corporate dashboard, badges, coins, ranks, professional accessories, childish mascot, exaggerated smile.
```

## В · «Видимый след»

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: exploratory art-direction concept sheet for a Telegram Mini App, direction C
Primary request: create one polished landscape concept sheet for an original youth community guide called Kontur. The bird is an independent urban companion that helps a person leave a visible useful trace in a community, not a cute pet, profession, or ranking avatar.
Scene/backdrop: bold editorial poster field made from black, warm white and one saturated color, with cropped route marks, torn-note edges and abstract conversation traces.
Subject: one original lean bird silhouette with oversized graphic wing and slightly awkward confident stance, expressive through posture. Show a large hero bird and four sequential poses: hanging back at the edge, stepping onto a marked path, pinning a useful fragment into a rough nest, sending a trace toward another bird-shaped shadow. Include a raw material/palette strip and a four-frame motion storyboard driven by quick cuts, stamped marks and a line that snaps into place.
Style/medium: contemporary independent magazine and cultural-poster illustration, risograph/screen-print, rough ink, cutout geometry, bold negative space, sophisticated youth culture.
Composition/framing: landscape presentation board, energetic asymmetrical grid but clear separation of hero, four states, material samples and motion frames; no device mockup and no readable words.
Lighting/mood: direct, witty, slightly rebellious, energetic but socially safe.
Color palette: warm white, dense black, vivid acid orange-red, dusty lavender, small sky-blue accent.
Constraints: original design only; no logos; no university marks; no text or letters; no watermark; same bird recognizable in every pose; strong mobile-scale silhouette.
Avoid: punk aggression, graffiti tags, corporate branding, game coins, ranks, crowns, professional accessories, childish mascot, kawaii, glossy 3D, fantasy nature scene, generic social-media icons.
```

## Проверка результата

- Во всех трёх листах есть крупная птица, четыре состояния, палитра и
  четырёхкадровая раскадровка.
- В изображениях нет логотипа ЦУ, текста, водяных знаков, профессиональных
  аксессуаров, валюты и рейтинга.
- Это растровые эскизы. После выбора направления птица и интерфейс должны быть
  заново спроектированы в управляемой графической системе.
