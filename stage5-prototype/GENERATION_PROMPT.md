# Запрос для уточнённой птицы

Способ: встроенный генератор изображений Codex.  
Входное изображение: `../art-direction/direction-b-signal-map.png` как
художественная основа.  
Результат: `assets/refined-bird-concept.png`.

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: refined Stage 5 character concept sheet for a Telegram Mini App prototype
Input images: Image 1 is the selected art-direction reference; preserve its crisp editorial geometry, mineral-gray field, cobalt/chartreuse/coral palette, subtle screen-print texture, route-line idea and strong small-scale silhouette.
Primary request: refine the same visual direction into a warmer, more compact bird companion. Redesign only the bird proportions and emotional tone: shorten the neck substantially, make the body compact and balanced, soften the beak angle, use a calm open eye and a slight curious head tilt. It should feel intelligent, approachable and quietly capable, never cute like a baby mascot.
Subject: one large anchor bird and four clearly consistent states of exactly the same bird: 1) observing a signal dot, 2) finding and holding one cobalt thread, 3) arranging the thread into a simple nest contour, 4) connecting the nest to a second coral node. Show a concise four-frame motion strip for dot -> line -> loop -> stable connection.
Style/medium: crisp editorial vector-like illustration with restrained screen-print grain; practical enough to translate into simple SVG and CSS motion.
Composition/framing: landscape concept sheet with large character, four state poses, five palette swatches and motion strip; generous whitespace; no phone mockup.
Color palette: warm chalk gray, deep navy, electric cobalt, softened chartreuse, warm coral; add a small warm off-white chest area to humanize the bird.
Constraints: preserve direction B, not direction A or C; no text or letters; no logos or university marks; no professional accessories; no ranking or game currency; no watermark; same bird proportions and markings in every state.
Avoid: long heron neck, sharp hostile expression, robot bird, owl cliché, generic round chick, childish cuteness, glossy 3D, dense network surveillance imagery, paper collage, aggressive poster style.
```
