# BIRD CHARACTER DIRECTION

Status: mandatory FINAL_TZ §10/§16 artifact for the current six-result candidate. This is an art-direction record, not a backend-taxonomy change. Backend roles remain `designer | coder | creative | manager | vibe`; `kotosov` remains a presentation-only `vibe` subtype.

## Shared visual rule

All six characters belong to one collectible family. They should be compact, slightly absurd/memetic, attractive rather than cheap-sticker-like, readable at thumbnail size, and recognisable by silhouette. Avoid photoreal animal portraiture, epic/totem poses, inconsistent rendering styles, or replacing character identity when applying a coloration.

Memness target: one clear exaggerated role trait per character, supported by pose/face/prop, without turning the result into a literal profession icon.

## Designer → Magpie

**IT archetype:** visually alert product/UI designer who notices composition, details and shiny possibilities before everyone else.

**Personality traits:** curious; selective; slightly perfectionistic/possessive about beautiful details.

**Memness level:** medium. The joke should come from the magpie's intense attraction to a tiny visually pleasing detail, not from costume overload.

**What is already strong:** the current reviewed `magpie.webp` has a readable compact bird silhouette and already belongs to the accepted result family.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** a neutral ornithological magpie with no visible attitude; excessive realism; generic “creative person” props that erase the bird character.

## Coder → Woodpecker

**IT archetype:** focused engineer who keeps hitting one technical problem until it yields.

**Personality traits:** concentrated; persistent; slightly overclocked.

**Memness level:** medium-high. A compact forward-leaning posture and an almost comically serious focus are enough; no hacker clichés are required.

**What is already strong:** the current reviewed `woodpecker.webp` gives the coder a distinct species/silhouette and remains the canonical master.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** hoodies, green-code backgrounds and generic cyber imagery; an aggressive battle-bird pose; photoreal wildlife framing.

## Creative → Jay

**IT archetype:** idea-generator who connects unexpected things quickly and brings energy to an unfinished concept.

**Personality traits:** expressive; restless; playful/confident.

**Memness level:** medium-high. The character may look as if it has just had an idea slightly faster than everyone else can process it.

**What is already strong:** the reviewed `jay.webp` has a visually distinct result identity and can carry a lively, colourful role without changing the existing scoring taxonomy.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** generic “artist” symbolism, rainbow overload, or a serious natural-history bird with no personality.

## Manager → Crane

**IT archetype:** organiser who keeps the group moving, sees the next task and projects calm control even when everything is messy.

**Personality traits:** collected; lightly self-important; practical/decisive.

**Memness level:** high but restrained. The managerial joke should be immediately readable in pose/face/one prop, while the bird stays elegant and collectible.

**What is already strong:** the current bounded redraw `crane-collectible.svg` fixes the rejected epic/totem direction with a compact body, tie and task tablet, while preserving a recognisable crane.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** a tall dangerous/majestic crane, long heroic legs dominating the frame, solemn “boss” portraiture, or a generic business mascot. This is the most explicit owner correction in the current art pass.

## Vibe → Owl

**IT archetype:** calm vibe-driven participant who reads the room, contributes selectively and can appear mysteriously competent without advertising the process.

**Personality traits:** observant; deadpan; relaxed.

**Memness level:** medium. The humour should come from composure/eyes/posture, not from turning the owl into a wizard or generic wise mascot.

**What is already strong:** the reviewed `owl.webp` is already a recognisable compact result master and remains the canonical ordinary `vibe` presentation.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** “wise owl” school/logo clichés, epic mystical lighting, or an interchangeable realistic owl portrait.

## Vibe subtype → Kotosov

**IT archetype:** вайбкодер: the same backend `vibe` result expressed as the deliberately more memetic cat-owl personality approved in the owner/zezokuk direction.

**Personality traits:** cheeky; self-assured; charmingly unserious.

**Memness level:** high. This is the strongest meme character of the set, but it still has to look like part of the same premium collectible family rather than an imported meme sticker.

**What is already strong:** the reviewed `kotosov.webp` gives the six-result presentation a memorable outlier while remaining client-local and deterministic by `player.userId`.

**What to avoid / what feels generic:** copying an internet reference literally; changing backend role taxonomy; replacing Kotosov with ordinary owl art in Home/drop/collection; stylistic mismatch with the other five masters.

## Cross-surface acceptance

For every result, hatch/reveal, result portrait, Home/profile, collection thumbnail and cosmetic coloration must preserve one recognisable character identity. Cropping/scale may change; species identity, head/body proportions, face/eyes, characteristic pose and art language must not.

The six canonical masters for the current candidate are recorded in `KONTUR_SIX_CHARACTER_BOARD_2026-08-18.md`. This direction document supplies the FINAL_TZ fields that board alone did not contain: role archetype, 2–3 visual personality traits, memness level, retained strength and the specific seriousness/genericity failure to avoid for each result.
